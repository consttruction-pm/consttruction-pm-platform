# Brand & Go-To-Market Plan v1.0

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Status:** Planning baseline  
**Scope:** Product brand, website launch, pre-launch demand generation, pre-sale, launch campaign, and post-launch growth.

## 1. Strategic objective

The product must enter the market as a specialized construction Project Management + Project Controls platform, not as a generic task manager.

The commercial release target remains governed by Product Principles:
1. Primavera P6 + PMBOK alignment.
2. Complete and forward-looking construction lifecycle/project-controls coverage.
3. One project truth.
4. Offline-first parity.
5. Accountable AI.
6. Typed interoperability.

Marketing must never claim a capability as available until it is release-certified.

## 2. Brand naming decision

### Rejected working name: PEYVARA / پیوارا

This name must not be used as the final public brand. Current web research found a strong collision with Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA), commonly rendered in Persian as «پیوارا». The collision creates avoidable confusion in search, brand identity, and future legal positioning.

### Naming requirements

The final name should:
- be short and easy to pronounce in Persian and English;
- work as a product, website, desktop application, and mobile application name;
- avoid generic terms such as Project, Build, Construct, Schedule, Control as the sole distinctive element;
- have a low apparent collision risk across software, construction, ERP, AI, and project-management markets;
- support a distinctive icon without relying on a literal building/crane/helmet;
- have plausible .com and key regional-domain availability;
- pass trademark, company-name, app-store, social-handle, and search-confusion screening before public commitment.

### Naming workflow

1. Generate 20-30 candidates.
2. Eliminate phonetic/semantic collisions.
3. Search software/company/app usage.
4. Screen domains and social handles.
5. Screen trademark databases in target launch markets.
6. Test Persian and English pronunciation with 5-10 target users.
7. Select one primary name plus one backup.
8. Reserve domains/handles before public announcement.
9. Add legal/trademark clearance before final registration.

No candidate is considered final from a brainstorming list alone.

## 10. Pricing and pre-sale

Initial commercial structure:
- Free/Demo entry for discovery.
- Professional for smaller teams/projects.
- Enterprise for larger companies and multi-project operations.
- Pilot package for high-friction enterprise adoption.

Founding customer benefits can include:
- launch pricing locked for a defined period;
- migration assistance;
- onboarding;
- training;
- priority support;
- controlled early-access features.

Do not use permanent deep discounts that devalue enterprise positioning.

## 11. Trust and evidence

Marketing claims must be traceable to:
- released feature;
- tested behavior;
- documented compatibility;
- customer-approved case study;
- or clearly marked roadmap status.

AI marketing must disclose:
- source/project context;
- human review where applicable;
- action approval;
- audit trail;
- limitations.
