# Free Market Acquisition Channels v1.0

Date: 2026-09-25
Scope: Free/organic promotion and customer acquisition only. Infrastructure and hosting are explicitly out of scope.

## 1. Goal
Acquire the first qualified construction-software users without buying advertising placements.

## 2. Channel classification

### A. High-priority buyer/discovery channels
1. LinkedIn — official company page plus founder/technical-lead profiles. Best for B2B construction/project-controls audiences. LinkedIn confirms Company Pages are free and support posts, analytics and a lead-oriented call to action.
2. G2 — maintain an organic software profile, product categories, screenshots, languages, pricing/trial information and verified reviews. G2's FY26 vendor pricing guide documents free profile features.
3. Capterra / GetApp — establish accurate organic vendor profiles. GetApp states that it lists all providers, while sponsored profiles are paid.
4. SaaSHub — evergreen software-discovery and alternatives listing.
5. AlternativeTo — especially relevant to the product's replacement strategy: Primavera P6 / MS Project / Excel alternative discovery.

### B. Launch-day/free exposure
6. Product Hunt — free to use and free product posting; use a personal maker account. Do not buy votes or ask for upvotes. Product Hunt explicitly prohibits paid/artificial voting.
7. Peerlist Launchpad — free launch path reported in current 2026 launch research; useful for technical/product audience.
8. Hacker News / Show HN — free, but only when the product is genuinely usable and technically interesting; do not use as a landing-page-only advertisement.
9. Indie Hackers — free founder/community participation, strongest when the post tells the build story, lessons, evidence and milestones instead of being a pure advertisement.
10. Dev.to / technical publishing — free technical articles and launch write-ups can build search visibility and credibility with software-aware users.

### C. Community-led construction acquisition
11. LinkedIn construction/project-controls groups and professional discussions.
12. Relevant Reddit communities for construction, project management, scheduling, Primavera/P6 and SaaS. Only participate where rules permit self-promotion.
13. Persian Telegram/WhatsApp professional communities focused on construction management, planning, project controls and contractors, using administrator-approved educational posts rather than spam.
14. Persian professional publishing/blog channels where construction planners and project managers already exchange templates and methods.

## 3. Do not prioritize
- Generic 'submit your startup to 500 directories' services.
- Low-quality backlink directories.
- Paid-only launch boards.
- Unrelated consumer marketplaces/classifieds.
- Mass-copying the same promotional message.
- Any channel that requires fake reviews, purchased votes or undisclosed sponsorship.

## 4. 2026 availability notes
- Product Hunt: free; company/brand accounts cannot post; personal account is required.
- Product Hunt recommends live products users can explore; an email-signup-only page is not eligible for homepage treatment.
- BetaList: current 2026 research reports free submission is no longer available; do not include it in the no-budget baseline.
- Uneed: current 2026 research reports its free queue is closed; do not depend on it for the launch schedule.

## 5. Recommended execution order
### Pre-launch
LinkedIn + YouTube + technical articles + construction communities + G2/Capterra/GetApp/SaaSHub/AlternativeTo profiles.

### 2-4 weeks before launch
Founder build-in-public posts + specialist educational series + first customer/pilot stories + launch-page preparation.

### Launch week
Product Hunt + LinkedIn + YouTube + community discussions + direct invitations to qualified contacts.

### Post-launch
Case studies + verified reviews + comparison pages + recurring technical education + referrals.

## 6. Content format by channel
LinkedIn: 3 specialist posts/week + 1 product demonstration/week.
YouTube: 1 substantial demo/education video every 1-2 weeks.
Reddit/communities: problem-solving answers and selected product demonstrations.
Product Hunt: one polished launch story with actual product access.
G2/Capterra/GetApp/SaaSHub/AlternativeTo: accurate evergreen product profiles.
Dev.to: deep technical articles around P6 compatibility, scheduling, EVM, construction control and AI accountability.

## 7. Free acquisition CTA
Pre-launch: Join Early Access.
Beta: Request a Pilot.
Public launch: Start a Project / Book a Demo.

## 8. Measurement
Use UTM tracking and record leads by channel.
Required channel metrics: visits, qualified leads, demo requests, pilots, paid conversions, activation and referral.
Do not judge a channel by traffic alone; qualified project opportunities are the primary outcome.

## 9. Zero-budget launch baseline
Core seven: LinkedIn, Product Hunt, G2, Capterra/GetApp, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, YouTube.
Community four: Reddit, Indie Hackers, relevant construction communities, Persian Telegram/WhatsApp groups.
Technical credibility two: Dev.to and Show HN when appropriate.

## 10. Governance
All claims must use released, tested product evidence or be clearly labelled roadmap.
Customer testimonials/reviews must be genuine and permission-based.
Comparison pages must be factual and version/date aware.
Community rules always take priority over promotional goals.
