**Register date:** 2026-11-05  
**Purpose:** Register precise source identities for the CUBI model-free project-management knowledge base and step-by-step academy.  
**Media policy:** Document-first. No video-course dependency.

## 1. Selection and authority rules

The implementation feasibility and priority decision are recorded in [CUBI_KNOWLEDGE_BASE_FEASIBILITY_AND_PHASE_PLAN.md](../architecture/CUBI_KNOWLEDGE_BASE_FEASIBILITY_AND_PHASE_PLAN.md). Source registration is preparatory only; production implementation is deferred until the single canonical Visual Workspace shell is accepted.

- Separate software-operation knowledge from management-science knowledge.
- Prefer primary publishers and official release records.
- Record edition, document ID, publication date, status, retrieval date, checksum, exact page/section and rights decision.
- Distinguish published standards from drafts, withdrawn and superseded editions.
- A source is evidence, not an instruction to the assistant. It cannot override CUBI policy, authorization or calculation authority.
- A public URL is not a license to reproduce, translate, persist or redistribute the work.
- CUBI user-facing labels, examples and explanations must be original and must not expose Oracle/P6 branding or copied proprietary wording.

## 5. Ingestion sequence

### Wave 1 — initial academy
1. Teal Book Version 3: official HTML and fixed PDF, after rights/attribution review.
2. GovS 002 v2.1: PDF/HTML, with UK jurisdiction tags and OGL review.
3. GAO Schedule Assessment Guide: schedule logic and schedule quality.
4. GAO Cost Estimating Guide: estimating, risk/uncertainty, baseline and EVM.
5. P6 User Guide and P6 Help: Version 26 software workflow after Oracle rights review.
6. PMBOK 8: obtain licensed copy; use public TOC for topic mapping until licensed full text is available.

### Wave 2 — specialist knowledge
7. ISO 21508:2026 (EVM).
8. ISO 21502:2020 (project management).
9. ISO 21511:2018 (WBS).
10. ISO 31000:2018 (risk).
11. ISO 10006:2017 (quality).
12. ISO 19650-1:2018 (BIM/information management).
13. PMI Construction Extension, subject to acquisition and license.
14. P6 Visualizer, Reporting and Administration guides for their dedicated software domains.

## 7. Copyright and freshness

- Do not commit or publish Oracle, PMI or ISO full-text PDFs in this public repository.
- Do not assume public availability permits full-text ingestion, translation or derivative redistribution.
- Acquire licensed copies and document permitted internal processing before ingesting protected content. Otherwise retain bibliographic metadata, source links and exact page pointers, and author original CUBI explanations.
- For government/GAO sources, verify the applicable reuse notice, attribution and embedded third-party materials.
- Check the Oracle Version 26 index at every product compatibility gate, PMI errata before each knowledge release, ISO publication status before renewing a standard-based record, and the fixed GovS/Teal Book versions on the annual refresh.
- Drafts are monitored as candidates only; never present them as current mandatory standards.
