# Product Scope

Construction project management and construction control platform.

## Governing principles
### Principle 1 — Primavera P6 + PMBOK alignment
For functionality overlapping Primavera P6, P6 logic is the compatibility baseline and calculations must be testable. PMBOK Guide Eighth Edition (November 2025) and ANSI/PMI 99-001-2025 guide governance, value delivery, scope, schedule, finance, stakeholders, resources, risk and procurement capabilities.

### Principle 2 — Complete modern Construction Management + Project Controls
After Principle 1, the product must cover the construction lifecycle from preconstruction and estimating through procurement, planning, field execution, commercial/change/claims, quality/safety, project controls, risk, closeout and portfolio management.

## Core capabilities
- Persian and English UI and project data.
- Jalali/Shamsi and Gregorian calendars.
- Enterprise P6-compatible scheduling: WBS, activities, Gantt, relationships, automatic scheduling, constraints, baselines, scenarios, float and criticality.
- Work calendars, duration/lag semantics, resources, costs, Progress, EVM and Earned Schedule.
- Excel import/export with typed numeric/date/duration fields.
- Web, Desktop and Mobile with offline/online parity.
- Remote daily reports, attendance, equipment and field updates.
- Documents, OCR, revisions, RFI, Submittal, correspondence, drawings, delay claims, evidence, workflow and audit trail.
- AI assistant, Smart Guide, voice-assisted entry and accountable AI actions.

## Modern construction completeness
### P0
AI Project Controls Copilot; cross-domain dependency graph; full field operations; Change/Variation/Notice/Claim workflow; portfolio/project control room; procurement/commercial commitments; ERP/accounting integration contracts.

### P1
Preconstruction estimating/takeoff/bid; BIM/CDE/4D/5D; site-reality/visual progress intelligence; quality; safety; commissioning/turnover/closeout.

### P2
GIS, weather, IoT, geofencing, supplier/subcontractor portal, SSO, e-signature, public API/integration marketplace, BI/data warehouse and benchmarking.

## Compatibility and non-regression
No product-completeness feature may replace or redefine Shared Core P6 scheduling, Calendar, Duration/Lag, Progress/EVM, Resource/Cost or financial semantics.
