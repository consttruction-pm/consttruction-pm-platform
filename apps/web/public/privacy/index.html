PRIVACY

Privacy in plain language

This page explains the data the product handles so you can decide what to share. It is a product description, not a legal opinion.

Account data

We keep what is needed to authenticate you and operate the workspace.

Identity

Account email, a salted password hash and the roles granted to the account.

Sessions

Session identifiers needed to keep you signed in, invalidated when you sign out.

Operational logs

Records used to secure the service and diagnose failures.

Project data

Project records belong to the tenant that created them.

Tenant isolation

A tenant's project records are not served to another tenant's accounts.

Membership

Within a tenant, only project members can read or write a project's records.

Public pages

Marketing pages are static and hold no project records.
