PAYMENTS

Clear billing, no surprises

CUBI Pro is purchased in monthly units. The server, not the page, confirms and records the entitlement after a verified payment.

How a purchase works

You choose the number of months, review the estimated total and continue to checkout. Nothing is charged by the page itself.

Monthly units

Twelve months means twelve monthly units purchased together, not a separate annual plan.

Existing subscriptions

A confirmed purchase extends the existing future expiry date instead of starting over.

No provider, no charge

If no verified provider is configured, checkout cannot start and no payment is taken.

Clarity and records

Prices and entitlements are recorded explicitly, so a later question can be answered from the record.

Working price

The configured working price is shown before you continue to checkout.

Server-side entitlement

Only the server records the entitlement; the browser cannot grant it.

Refunds and questions

Contact us with the account and payment reference for any billing question.
