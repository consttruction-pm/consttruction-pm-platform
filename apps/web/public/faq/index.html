FAQ

Questions we hear most

Short, direct answers about accounts, environments, data handling and payments.

Accounts and environments

Do I need two accounts?

No. One account can open both environments when it carries the roles each environment requires.

Why does Enterprise ask for a role?

Enterprise exposes organisation-level surfaces. Access follows the role, and the server enforces it.

Can I switch environment later?

Yes. The environment selector is the entry point and shows only the environments your account is authorised for.

Data, security and payment

Where is my project data stored?

In the account's project database. Public marketing pages never hold project records.

Who can see a project?

Only accounts with membership of that project, within the same tenant.

When is a payment taken?

Only after a configured payment provider confirms it. If checkout cannot start, no payment is made.
