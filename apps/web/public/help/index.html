HELP CENTER

Help when the work needs to continue

Most questions are about access: signing in, choosing the right environment and opening the right project. Start here, then use the in-product help for the window you are in.

Common questions

The quickest answers to the situations that stop work.

I cannot sign in

Check the account email and whether the account is active. Repeated failed attempts are throttled for safety.

Enterprise is disabled for me

Enterprise requires an enterprise role on your account. Ask your administrator to grant it; the selector explains the reason.

I cannot see my project

Projects are scoped by membership. Ask the project owner to add your account.
